1. Definition & Functional Purpose of Cookies

Cookies are compact text identifiers stored on your computing device or mobile terminal by your internet browser when interacting with digital portals. At ScalarReadIvy, we employ functional and analytical cookies to facilitate seamless navigation, preserve active diagnostic session tokens, and recall scholar interface preferences. These digital mechanisms enable our server architecture to distinguish your session from other visitors without retaining unnecessary personally identifiable dossiers.

Functional cookies deployed across our website are strictly necessary to deliver core digital services, such as retaining your selected seminar course track, enrollment timestamps, and accessibility visual configurations as you navigate between pages.
