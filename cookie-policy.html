1. Definition & Functional Purpose of Cookies

Cookies are compact text identifiers stored on your computing device or mobile terminal by your internet browser when interacting with digital portals. At BlousePoiseJet, we employ functional and analytical cookies to facilitate seamless navigation, preserve active fitting consultation tokens, and recall client preferences. These digital mechanisms enable our server architecture to distinguish your session from other visitors without retaining unnecessary personally identifiable dossiers.
